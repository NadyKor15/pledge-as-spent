# Money engine: decisions where SPEC-core left room

Conservative readings taken while building the engine (23.10.2026; 7-11, 17, 18 revised the same day after an acceptance review). Each one is covered by a test.

1. **Day count.** Day = whole days since the original authorization (day 0 = first 24 h). Reauthorize from day 4 (>= 96 h, later than PayPal's "after the 3-day honor period", so never `REAUTHORIZATION_TOO_SOON`); expiry at day 29 (29 x 24 h); renewal link from day 26; capture trigger at <= 48 h before expiry.
2. **Reauthorize.** Only PayPal-account holds of an open drive, not revoked, not while a capture is in flight, with something uncaptured left. Amount = authorized - captured. One per hold from the planner; a second only via `planExplicitReauthorize`. A returned id is chained; commands use the newest.
3. **Whole quantities.** `needQty` and `qtyNeeded` must be positive integers; anything else is a `CoreError` (the need's unit should make counts whole: bottle, pack).
4. **Partly unfunded line.** `qtyCovered` grows by the accepted quantity even when pledges do not cover the money: the goods were bought. The gap is `unfundedBase` for the coordinator; the payout covers funded money only.
5. **Several match holds** (a renewal overlapping the old hold) are allowed only at the drive's ratio; the match part is split across them by capacity, like donors.
6. **Added event `PledgeRevoked`** (not in the SPEC list): without it a withdrawn pledge would keep funding lines until its void. Revoked: no new shares, accrued shares are captured, then void.
7. **One capture in flight per hold.** A pending capture older than 10 minutes (option) is planned again with the same request id. `CaptureFailed` means PayPal refused; a timeout is a retry, not a failure.
   **Refusals are classified** (`refusals.ts`, issue names from the schema's error list; panel K2). Terminal (`AUTHORIZATION_VOIDED/EXPIRED/DENIED/ALREADY_CAPTURED`, `PREVIOUSLY_CAPTURED/VOIDED`, `INSTRUMENT_DECLINED`, `CARD_EXPIRED`, `PAYER_ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED/LOCKED_OR_CLOSED`, `PAYER_CANNOT_PAY`, `PAYEE_ACCOUNT_RESTRICTED`, `TRANSACTION_REFUSED`, `MAX_CAPTURE_COUNT/AMOUNT_EXCEEDED`, `AUTH_CAPTURE_CURRENCY_MISMATCH`), or the 3rd refusal in a row: the hold is *blocked*, its batch and everything accrued on it become `uncollected` with the reason, it funds nothing more, and it is voided. Anything else (5xx, `INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR`, `RATE_LIMIT_REACHED`, unknown text such as `capture X DECLINED`): back to accrued, next seq after 15 min, then 30 min. The coordinator's force skips the wait, not the bound. `CaptureFailed.terminal` overrides the text when the caller knows better.
8. **Close.** `planClose` needs `DriveClosing` first. Each hold is voided once its own captures are confirmed (no drive-wide wait). `DriveClosed` needs every hold settled; payouts may still be pending.
   **A donor may approve after the close** (panel K1): the authorization is a PayPal fact and is recorded. Once the drive is not open, `planHoldMaintenance` and `planClose` (also on a *closed* drive) void every live hold that still holds money, so `unsettledHolds` returns to empty. The service's `advance()` must therefore also plan for closed drives (it returns [] for them today, `src/server/service.ts:657`).
9. **Uncollected is its own bucket, not part of released** (panel K5; changed 23.10, NOT "uncollected inside released"). Shares the hold could not collect (expiry, void, terminal refusal) stay `uncollected` with a reason; `released` is only money let go unused. Every hold: authorized = captured + capturePending + accrued + uncollected + reversed + released + uncommitted. `closeReport` shows pledged / spent / released / uncollected / reversed, settled when they add up; the statement's `totals.charged` is what the donor actually paid (`totals.share` stays "all shares, charged or not").
10. **Payout retries and top-ups.** First attempt is exactly `pay:{driveId}:{receiptId}`; later attempts are `pay:{driveId}:{receiptId}:{n}`, because PayPal rejects a reused `sender_batch_id`. Lines of a receipt funded after its payout was confirmed (a flagged line approved later) are paid as a top-up of the difference (panel K4, confirmed by test).
11. **Fixed fees.** USD 0.49, EUR 0.39 and JPY 49 are measured from sandbox captures on 23.10 (`docs/platform-findings.md` #4); GBP, CAD, AUD, HUF, TWD stay `estimate: true`. Savings count confirmed batches only.
12. **Currency list.** `CurrencyCode` is unchanged (other modules may map over it); HUF and TWD exponents are handled by code string in `money.ts`.
13. **Rounding.** "Half-up" is half away from zero. A share worth less than half a minor unit of the donor's currency is funded in base but charges nothing (`nothing_to_capture`). Per share, the charge differs from the base share by at most one minor unit of the hold.
14. **Upstream refusals** are recorded with `rejectLine` as `LineRejected`, so the book shows every line of a receipt; a refused line can never be funded later.
15. **Time** is passed in as zoned ISO strings only; a zoneless string would be read in server-local time.
16. **Story fixture** puts the beer at line 9 and the gift card at line 11. Align with `eval/receipts/hero/README.md` once it exists.
17. **Reversals** (panel K3). `CaptureReversed` applies only to a confirmed batch, found by capture id (`captureReversedEvent(state, { captureId, amount, reason, at, reversalId? })` for the webhook handler; null if the capture is not ours). The amount moves from `captured` to `reversed`, is split over the batch's shares by largest remainder (statement status `reversed` / `partly_reversed`), may come in parts up to the captured amount, and a repeated `reversalId` counts once. Hold status is left as it was (PayPal does not reopen the authorization). The service should map `PAYMENT.CAPTURE.REVERSED` / `REFUNDED` to it; `CaptureFailed` on a confirmed batch is still refused.
18. **Honor period** (panel K6). Holds stay fundable until day 29, card or PayPal account: stopping at the 3-day honor period would idle a PayPal-account hold for most of its life too (one reauthorize buys 3 more days). Each share records `honored: false` when funded after `honorUntil`; views show it. If such a capture is later refused, decision 7 makes the loss visible as `uncollected` instead of stalling the hold.
19. **Parent and child of a reauthorization** (live probe 2026-10-31, `evidence/reauth-probe-20261031T223634Z.json`, NOT "all further commands go to the newest id"). Captures go to the newest id; both ids accept them and both show PARTIALLY_CAPTURED. Void and reauthorize go to the parent: a void of the child answers 422 `CANNOT_BE_VOIDED`, a void of the parent ends the child too, a reauthorize of the child answers 422 `REAUTHORIZATION_NOT_SUPPORTED`, of the parent `REAUTHORIZATION_TOO_SOON` right after the first one. A reauthorize does not extend the 29 days (`expiration_time` stays) and works on a partly captured hold for the amount left. The service treats only `PREVIOUSLY_VOIDED` and `AUTHORIZATION_VOIDED` as "already gone": `CANNOT_BE_VOIDED` keeps the hold alive in the book.
